# Independent review of attempt 02

## Scope and source checks

This attempt estimates only Equation (1) on physical page 19: pooled first-year XRET on the annual descending rank of ΔRDA, ascending rank of RDA, and ascending rank of ARNOA. Each rank takes values 0–4 divided by four. The source reports the coefficient and t-statistic but no equation-specific N, estimator, or standard-error method.

Footnote 16 on physical page 24 places the return interval from the fifth month of fiscal year `t+1` through the fourth month of `t+2`. The code’s month offsets 5–16 from the fiscal-year `t` statement implement that interval and preserve the 1999 accounting/2000 return support needed for the latest sample years.

I checked the matching instructions on physical pages 6–7 against footnote 5 on physical page 24. Footnote 5 defines benchmark-constituent formation using prior-December size, prior-calendar-year accounting data, and June 30 NYSE breakpoints. Separately, pages 6–7 say to match the target at the investment date using its current size and book-to-market against the preceding June 30 cutoffs, then update the target assignment on subsequent June 30 dates. A1 joined targets directly to annual `port_assign` membership, so its initial match used the benchmark constituents’ lagged inputs. That was a concrete source deviation.

In A2, the initial target match uses the sample observation’s complete company MVE at the end of month four after fiscal year-end and `(CEQ + TXDITC + RDASSET)` from that fiscal-year statement divided by MVE4. The target is matched to the cutoffs for the preceding June 30. Return months use the corresponding July–June portfolio year, so a June update applies to July returns. For an active target at a June 30 update, A2 uses complete June company market equity and the latest annual statement whose assumed availability date has arrived by June 30. The assumed date is the first day of the fifth month after `DATADATE`, consistent with the paper’s investment-date convention; the statement’s adjusted book is divided by June market equity and compared with that June’s cutoffs. The paper gives no statement release dates or refreshed B/M formula, so this is an explicit four-month publication-delay approximation. The selected accounting date, market date, and cutoff year are carried in the handoff.

The delisting path now takes the last initial/June target assignment available by the delisting month. A target delisted before June 30 is not assigned again at that date; its investment-date group supplies post-delisting benchmark returns. Of 33,170 initial target matches, 32,230 are assigned and 940 lack adjusted book. The build identifies 32,460 possible June updates; it suppresses 840 for targets delisted by the update date. Of the remaining 31,620 active updates, 30,626 are assigned; 908 lack as-of adjusted book, 47 lack current company market equity, and 39 lack a unique CCM company link. The bounded SAS and Stata smoke run checked an active June update with unequal current and later book values, confirmed that a March statement unavailable until August was excluded, and confirmed that a target delisted before June retained its initial group for July returns.

The R&D asset and amortization formulas match physical page 5 (`XRD_t + .8XRD_{t−1} + .6XRD_{t−2} + .4XRD_{t−3} + .2XRD_{t−4}`; amortization is `.2` times the five preceding XRD values). Note 4 on physical page 23 permits missing/immaterial XRD on an observed statement to be treated as zero; the build does not invent observations for absent fiscal years. The corrected return construction is followed by fresh within-year ranks before the joint complete-case regression screen. The handoff has 25,741 nonmissing ΔRDA values, 33,147 RDA values, and 25,715 ARNOA values for rank formation; all three rank variables are then used in the 24,922-observation Eq. (1) fit.

## Other construction checks

The benchmark-constituent logic remains separate from target matching. It selects annual accounting by actual `DATADATE` calendar year and maps a statement in year `y−1` to formation year `y`, uses prior-December company size, forms independent NYSE quintile cutoffs at June 30, and retains the 25 value-weighted July–June portfolios. The final listing has 25 populated portfolios and 300 portfolio-month observations in every formation year 1979–2000, including returns through June 2001.

I rechecked the remaining predecessor repairs in the final code and listing. Company market equity is the complete sum of common issues (share codes 10/11) active on the last DSI trading date; an expected issue without observed market equity leaves that company-month unavailable rather than producing a partial sum. Of 1,698,747 company-months, 35,515 have an issue without market equity; no company-month has a missing issue row, PERMCO disagreement, or name-history conflict, and no common issue is unattributed. Portfolio accounting-year duplicates and delisting-only return rows are separately diagnosed.

Annual flows use Xpressfeed `EXRAT12M`; balance-sheet inputs use paired `EXRATD` rates on the statement date or the latest paired rate at most seven days earlier. For the 17,413 CAD statement rows, 14,746 have an annual flow rate, 17,189 have converted XRD because source-authorized zero XRD needs no quote, and 14,734 have a daily stock rate (14,173 exact and 561 prior-date). No conflicting currency-rate groups or mixed daily rate types are reported. The build retains only observed fiscal-year rows in the R&D history; missing XRD on an observed row is set to zero under note 4, while missing years do not create lag observations.

NOA follows the cited Nissim–Penman decomposition, not a generic assets-minus-liabilities proxy. I checked physical page 41 of the local cited paper: `NOA = NFO + CSE + MI`; the preferred treasury and arrears terms cancel, giving `DLC + DLTT + PSTK − CHE − IVAO + CEQ + MIB`. A2 retains signed NOA and requires only nonzero denominators where the ratios are formed; no positive-NOA screen was added. R&D capitalization/amortization uses the source weights, and the handoff retains pre-shift lag values and dates used in the calculations.

Delisting support has 13,927 event-month keys with no duplicate or conflicting DLRET; 12,439 filtered DLRET-only event rows are retained, 10,950 map to a common PERMCO, and 10,879 have prior-month market equity. The completed return windows use observed RET with DLRET when available, then substitute the control portfolio after delisting. Of 33,170 screened R&D firm-years, 31,959 have complete twelve-month excess-return windows; 1,028 windows lack a target group and benchmark return. Those assignment gaps are reflected in the complete-case sample rather than zero-filled.

After the full run, I audited those 12,439 filtered DLRET-only keys against raw CRSP MSF and effective-dated MSENAMES in a bounded native SAS pass (`preflight/dlret_raw_audit.sas`; aggregate output in `preflight/dlret_raw_audit.lst`). All 12,439 have a raw MSF row for the event month, while 134 have nonmissing raw RET. There are 10,950 keys with a unique PERMCO and common share code at DLSTDT; for each, the raw month-end MSF date is after the common-share name interval and RET is missing. None of those 10,950 has a nonmissing RET, and none of the 134 nonmissing raw RET observations has a unique dated common-share link at DLSTDT. The filter therefore explains the raw-row/name-date mismatch, but this audit found no ordinary return with both nonmissing RET and a sound event-date common link to combine with DLRET. I left the existing DLRET-only return unchanged; the 134 unlinked raw returns remain an unresolved security-attribution limitation. The audit had no SAS errors or warnings. No full build rerun was needed because this follow-up found no supported return correction.

## Results and sample

The SAS handoff contains 33,147 firm-years from 5,043 firms for 1979–1998. Stata’s complete-case Equation (1) sample is 24,922 observations. The paper’s 35,841 R&D firm-years are a parent-sample count, not an Equation (1) N; the source does not report regression N.
