## Evidence and route rules

The assigned article is `papers/pdf/W3124365414.pdf`. Equation (6) and its regressors are on physical p. 15; Table 4 and its reported estimates are on physical p. 17 (printed p. 351); the source-variable definitions are in Appendix A, physical pp. 31–33. The paper reports 21,758 Table 4 firm-years and SUSPECT_NI coefficients of −0.0200, −0.0591, and +0.0497 for abnormal CFO, discretionary expenses, and production costs.

The frozen catalog at `output/feasibility_runs/resources/catalog.json` and `/storage/sas/data_description.txt` labels `FUNDA.IID` “Header Primary Global Issue ID” and `FUNDA.HIID` “Historic Primary Global Issue ID” (catalog lines around 23,873 and 23,891). `FUNDA.PRCC_F` is labeled “Year End Close Price of Company's Primary Issue of Stock” (data description around line 31,092). The same local catalog describes `SEC_MTH.PRIMISS` as a Primary/Joiner flag, `SEC_MTHPRC.PRCCM` as monthly close price with `CURCDM`, and `SEC_DPRC.PRCCD` as daily close price with `CURCDD`. Their documented formats are `PRCCM F20.4` and `PRCCD F29.8`; annual `PRCC_F` is `F25.12`. The 2004 Research Insight guide’s A199/`PRCC_F` entry (indexed at physical pp. 674–675, printed pp. 624–625) defines fiscal-year close, notes that a suspended security uses its last trading close, and says historical Canadian `$T` prices are CAD. The 2013 provider CCM guide (indexed in `output/reproduction_runs/resource_documentation.json`, physical p. 8) defines `P` as Compustat primary and `J` as joiner for its primary/joiner field; applying those meanings to `SEC_MTH.PRIMISS` is supported by that field’s local “Primary/Joiner flag” label.

The route uses the full `HIID` without stripping identifier suffixes. A monthly quote is eligible only on the same GVKEY, HIID, and actual annual `DATADATE`, when the fiscal date is month-end, `PRCCM` agrees with nonmissing `PRCC_F`, and `CURCDM` is known. The marker is joined at that exact issue/date; a later month-end marker is not carried backward. A dated `P` on HIID corroborates the route. An explicit dated `J` or another explicit non-`P` value on HIID blocks it. If HIID has no marker, matching same-issue/date price and currency evidence can support it. The number of `P` issues for the company/date is reported as a diagnostic; it does not veto a directly supported HIID. If HIID is absent, a unique dated `P` issue is required, so multiple P issues remain ambiguous.

An unresolved route can use the nearest prior daily quote on the same GVKEY/HIID no more than three calendar days before the endpoint. An exact-date daily quote needs no gap check. A nonzero gap requires zero CRSP market sessions after the quote through the annual endpoint, using `CRSP.DSI` (`CRSPSTK.DSI` in the SAS library; catalog label “Stock - Market Indexes Daily NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ/ARCA”). The quote must also match annual `PRCC_F` and have one known `CURCDD`. The three-day cap is a conservative short-gap rule; no security-level suspension field is present in `SEC_DPRC`. The selected monthly or daily quote date supplies the `EXRATD` date for price translation. Accounting flows and balance-sheet FX dates remain as in A3.

The comparison tolerance is half of the precision displayed by the local price formats: 0.00005 for four-decimal monthly `PRCCM`, and 0.000000005 for eight-decimal daily `PRCCD`. These are explicit numerical matching bounds; SAS display formats alone do not establish stored numeric precision. The observed differences below quantify which comparisons they admit. In the 1986–2000 source endpoints, 127,707 same-issue/date monthly comparisons had 127,480 exact numeric matches, 195 nonzero differences within 0.00005, and 32 over that bound. The accepted monthly routes are exactly 127,675: 127,480 exact and 195 within precision; their maximum absolute difference is 0.000047160. Across the bounded nearest-daily candidate comparisons, 87,140 were exact, 37,628 exceeded the daily tolerance, and none had a nonzero difference within the tolerance. All 77 selected daily routes had exact numeric price matches. The over-bound monthly candidates were not accepted as monthly routes; the daily rule was separately evaluated where eligible.

## Route audit results

The price audit covered 130,164 nonmissing annual `PRCC_F` endpoints in fiscal years 1986–2000. It found 11,233 differences between header IID and historic HIID, 127,945 exact-date HIID markers equal to `P`, no HIID `J` markers, no explicit marker conflicts, and no same-date monthly/daily currency conflicts. It found 2,835 company/date groups with more than one dated P issue. Because `SEC_MTH` issue/date keys are checked for uniqueness before this count, those groups represent multiple issue rows rather than duplicate marker rows.

The direct HIID route retained 2,831 of those multi-P groups: HIID itself was marked `P`, and the same-date HIID price and currency matched (`2,063` CAD and `768` USD). Four multi-P groups did not meet the HIID price/currency support rule and were not selected by a multi-P fallback. The route therefore preserves a known HIID when its own dated evidence agrees instead of treating a second company/date P issue as an automatic contradiction. Among the accepted no-marker HIID daily routes, 23 also had one P marker on another issue; their same-HIID daily price and currency evidence supported the route. In total, 69 no-marker HIID daily routes and eight HIID/P daily routes were accepted, alongside the monthly routes.

The resulting source-key price currency coverage is 127,752/130,164, leaving 2,412 unsupported and unavailable. Routes comprise 127,675 monthly, 49 exact-date daily, and 28 daily closure routes with a zero-session gap. Same-date price currencies never conflict. Relative to A3’s unverified header-IID route, 28 old routes are not verified by the new rule, 451 are newly supported, and 1,932 source keys have a different currency. These are source-key counts, not final model attrition.

The weekend-session audit identified 27 price-agreeing Saturday endpoints whose nearest daily quote was Thursday and for which Friday appears as an open session in CRSP.DSI. None was accepted as a daily closure quote. All 27 instead passed an independent same-date HIID/P monthly price route (`HIID_P_M`), so the daily-candidate rejection did not remove their price currency. The complete 27-key and quote-price listing is in the final SAS listing at `runtime/20261102T232228493004Z/build.lst`.

## Remaining limitations

- The source-key audit leaves 2,412 annual prices without supported currency. Those values remain unavailable rather than inheriting currency from another issue or period.
- CRSP.DSI reports market-wide NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ/ARCA sessions, not a security-specific session or suspension indicator. Direct issue/date price matching and zero intervening market sessions narrow the daily closure route, but do not prove why that security lacks an intervening quote.
- The paper does not identify the annual cross-sectional estimator or Newey–West bandwidth. This attempt retains annual OLS and Newey–West lag 1 from A3.
- Equation (5) and Appendix A use lagged sales for normal discretionary expenses, while the Table 2 note prints contemporaneous sales. The construction continues to follow Equation (5) and Appendix A.
- The 1987 handoff contains 296 rows after the minimum group screen; 554 rows meet Appendix completeness before that screen, across 10 qualifying groups. Omitting the t−2 sales requirement adds nine complete rows. The local source’s early-period availability and the published sample difference remain unresolved.
