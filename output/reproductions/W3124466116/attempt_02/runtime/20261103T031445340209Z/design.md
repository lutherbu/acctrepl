# Table 4, column (2): reproduction design

## Source and model

The retained fit is equation (2), Table 4 column (2), a pooled OLS regression of worldwide current effective tax rate on SIZE, PTI, FOROPER, FOROPER squared, MNC, MNC×SIZE, MNC×PTI, 12 foreign-location indicators, six SIC groups, and year indicators. The source is the original PDF, physical pages 11–13, 15–18, and footnotes 10, 12, 13, and 18 on physical pages 25–26. Table 4 reports conventional OLS t-statistics but does not state an alternative covariance estimator or clustering rule; I use Stata's ordinary OLS standard errors and report two-sided p-values as required by the output template.

## Fundamentals, sample screens, and transformations

The input is `COMP.FUNDA`, restricted to annual `INDL` / `STD` / consolidated (`C`) domestic-population (`D`) records, fiscal years 1990–1997. The current Funda currency code must be USD so the paper's added $1 million is on the same unit scale as sales and pretax income. The sample is U.S.-incorporated (`FIC=USA`). Legacy annual items are mapped from the coordinator's provider index: #12→SALE, #170→PI, #63→TXFED, and #64→TXFO. The Research Insight definitions at physical pages 205–206 state that TXFED and TXFO are current taxes payable, both in millions of dollars; WWETR is `(TXFED+TXFO)/PI`. Both inputs must be observed; a missing component is not treated as zero. `TXT` or `TXT-TXDI` is not used.

The ETR pool applies the paper's Table 2 exclusions in order: foreign incorporation; zero total assets or pretax income; negative total assets or parent stockholders' equity; missing current ETR components; banks, insurance carriers, and utilities; pretax income or current tax expense at or below zero; then deletion of the pooled top and bottom 1% of `ROA=PI/AT`. WWETR values above one are recoded to one. Missing SIC rows remain in the ETR pool, consistent with Table 3 and footnote 18. SIZE and PTI use `ln(1+SALE)` and `ln(1+PI)` in USD millions, as footnote 10 specifies. The catalog labels `SICH` as historical SIC, so it is used for the date-specific industry screen and controls. Because the paper gives no exact SIC codes for the Table 2 sector exclusion, the 1987 SIC manual's major groups are used as a narrow operational mapping: utilities 4900–4999, depository institutions 6000–6099, and insurance carriers 6300–6399. This excludes those named source sectors without treating the entire finance, insurance, and real estate division (6000–6999) as banks or insurers. The Table 4 flag requires nonmissing SIC because footnote 18 says missing-SIC observations are excluded from Tables 4–6. SIC groups use nonoverlapping intervals `[0,2000)`, `[2000,3000)`, `[3000,4000)`, `[4000,5000)`, `[5000,6000)`, `[6000,8000]`, and `>8000`; the last group is the reference, consistent with Table 3's displayed boundaries and standard four-digit SIC classes. Footnote 18's 2,987 missing-SIC rows explain why the source regression N is below its 19,737 ETR pool; neither count is used to tune selection screens.

## Geographic construction and assumptions

The only segment-specific annual source in the catalog is `COMPSEG.WRDS_SEGMERGED`. It is joined to Funda on exact GVKEY and DATADATE, then restricted to the observed `GEOSEG` method. Provider documentation distinguishes `STYPE` (segment method) from `GEOTP` (geographic segment type): GEOTP 1 is Total Foreign, 2 is Domestic, and 3 is NonDomestic. GEOTP is not the legacy GAREAG country/region code. `IAS` is the current identifiable-assets field corresponding most closely to the paper's geographic segment assets; Funda `AT` is the total-assets denominator. The latest source date for each firm-date geographic report is retained. Source-date counts, SID uniqueness across source dates, latest-vintage duplicate SIDs, type/currency changes, and `CURCDS`/`ISOSRC` are audited. Amount records require `CURCDS=USD`, observed `SID`, and observed source date. A unique Total Foreign row is used alone and is never added to type-3 rows. If it is absent, observed NonDomestic component IAS values are summed when all component amounts are in USD, current SIDs are unique, source dates are observed, and a token-level audit finds no repeated country or broad-region/country hierarchy overlap. An explicit Domestic row is not required to sum foreign components; source `GEOTP=3` already identifies the rows as non-domestic. A single non-domestic row has no within-report pair to overlap, so its amount may be retained even if its name is unavailable; the location controls remain unclassified. A domestic-only observation can receive zero foreign assets only when the selected geography report explicitly has a current Domestic row and no foreign row. Absence of a segment report, unknown assets, duplicate SIDs, ambiguous multi-component names, hierarchy overlap, or incompatible currency remains missing. Reported foreign-assets-to-total-assets ratios above one are retained and reported; no cap or outlier exclusion is applied to them.

The current merged file has no SGEOCD/SGEOAR country-area fields. The catalog's `WRDS_SEG_CUSTOMER` has geographic fields for customers, which the provider documents as customer locations; they are not a substitute for operating-segment geography. The 12 source LOCATION indicators therefore use audited, explicit `SNMS` name matches for the reported regions/countries listed in Table 3 and footnote 12. Country aliases are normalized to the paper's controls; broad names such as Europe, Asia, or North America are checked against their separately reported country controls and are not treated as evidence that an unmentioned country is absent. Unrecognized names, empty names, and aggregate-only foreign coverage remain explicit as unclassified. Location flags are filled only when the selected report supports both observed presence and absence; no report or unclassified coverage does not become twelve zeros. This name-based reconstruction is best effort; it is not treated as a historical GAREAG crosswalk. MNC is one when observed foreign pretax income (`PIFO`) is positive or a supported foreign-asset measure is positive, zero only when both measures are observed and nonpositive, and missing otherwise. Location controls use component names, not GEOTP numeric values. No missing asset or geography amount is filled with zero.

## Scope and reporting

The one declared SAS/Stata file preserves the Table 2 ETR pool and carries a separate Table 4 complete-case flag. This permits sample-pool descriptive comparisons and the assigned fit to use their source-defined populations. Descriptives are reported for the ETR pool and for Table 4 complete cases, by MNC status where status is observed. The regression table reports coefficient estimates and template two-sided p-values; the source reports t-statistics and uses a mix of one- and two-sided stars. The SAS listing records sequential sample attrition, tax-item and currency diagnostics, segment type/name/vintage and SID audits, component hierarchy overlaps, foreign asset ratio distributions, ratios above one, and geography that remains unclassified. Any sample-size or coefficient discrepancy is reported from the observed logs and exported tables, not repaired by tuning screens.

## Executed result and data limits

The native run retained 21,706 rows in the ETR pool, 1,969 more than the paper's reported 19,737; 67 retained rows have missing SIC, so the input screen also differs from the paper's 2,987 missing-SIC observations. The model's complete-case flag contains 176 rows, compared with the paper's 16,750. This large shortfall follows the available segment source: among 25,960 GEOSEG NonDomestic rows, 24,055 have no segment name and 13,234 have no IAS. No Total Foreign GEOTP row is observed. The final file has a supported foreign-asset measure for 5,330 observations (4,181 component sums and 1,149 explicit Domestic-only zeros), but only 1,382 observations have fully observed location controls; 20,315 are unclassified and 19,317 have aggregate-only coverage. Latest component SIDs and source dates are complete and unique, while 163 firm-dates have 228 detected country/region hierarchy overlaps and are excluded from component sums. Four retained foreign-asset ratios exceed one; the maximum is 1.6384.

The exported Table 4 fit therefore does not reproduce the paper's coefficients on a comparable sample. In the candidate N=176 regression, FOROPER is 0.033 (p=.920), FOROPER squared is 0.075 (p=.892), and MNC is 0.292 (p=.022), versus source estimates −0.0706, 0.1057, and −0.0633 on N=16,750. The constrained geography fields and incomplete segment records prevent a like-for-like Table 4 reconstruction; these results are retained as a transparent best-effort run, not a source-matching result.
