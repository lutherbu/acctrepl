## Construction checks and repair

The corresponding implementation points checked were `build.sas` lines 10–89 (FX rates), 91–273 (Compustat rows, adjacency, and ratios), 274–358 (dated CCM links), 359–545 (CRSP capitalization, target ranks, and the repaired cap-date gate), 546–581 (MSIX), 582–789 (stock returns, delistings, and complete windows), and 790–845 (pooled trim and final file). The direct estimation and export commands are in the two code cells of `stata.ipynb`; both reload the common `reg.dta` and clear stored estimates. `submission.json` records the source tables, sample key, two assigned fits, and documented best-effort assumptions.

The formulas use Compustat `IB`, `OB`, and `AT`. Current earnings and backlog use the arithmetic mean of actual adjacent `AT` values at *t−1* and *t*; lead earnings use their own *t* and *t+1* asset average. `OB` is treated as a fiscal-year-end stock. Currency compatibility is checked separately for each ratio: same-currency pairs remain native; supported mixed pairs use the documented common-USD cross-rate. Daily rates apply to dated assets and `OB`; the statement-month 12-month rate applies to `IB`. The updated mixed-currency handling does not require unrelated FX horizons or a USD-only sample. There is no added OANCF, IBES, control, industry, or positive-only backlog screen.

The source code uses actual DATADATE/FYEAR keyed joins for adjacent statements and preserves gaps. In the linked final sample, current DATADATE runs from 30 June 1981 through 31 May 2000; all lead statements are present, including 155 FY1999 lead DATADATEs in calendar 2001. The 12-month stock and benchmark windows are complete through 31 August 2001. This confirms the required endpoint; the build does not shorten the source horizon to make counts resemble the paper.

CCM links are checked at fiscal year-end and the formation date with open date bounds retained. Best-ranked link ties that remain ambiguous are not arbitrarily collapsed. A bounded audit found no ambiguous best formation links among the reviewed final rows. One row changes PERMNO and PERMCO between formation and event dates; the available audit does not establish its specific cause. Eight FYE links expire before the holding start: four have another active CCM link for the same PERMCO and four have no active CCM link. All eight still have complete CRSP event returns. These are documented identity/availability limitations; no blanket CCM-at-start screen was imposed.

The event return starts in month four after fiscal year-end and compounds 12 monthly CRSP total returns. Valid `RET` and `DLRET` are combined; a delisting-only month with a valid terminal return is included, and later months are zero-return cash. Missing or ambiguous delisting proceeds and incomplete monthly stock/benchmark coverage remain unavailable. The build diagnostics show no duplicate monthly security returns, duplicate decile-month returns, ambiguous delisting-only month, or delisting-only month lacking a valid `DLRET` in the retained construction.

The target decile is reconstructed because MSIX supplies decile returns but not dated security membership. Current code selects eligible NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ non-ADR securities, ranks capitalization by return year, and merges the dated rank onto the event security. This is an approximation of CRSP's vendor product and its tie/new-entrant handling. The paper describes a buy-and-hold mean return for the size-matched decile; the local measure compounds MSIX monthly value-weighted total returns, which are annually rebalanced. Calendar-year-end rank formation may differ from the paper's fiscal-year-end wording for non-December fiscal years.

The independent cap-date audit established a real timing defect in the inherited fallback. The prior rule allowed any first eligible capitalization in the return year to determine the event target decile, even when that cap came after the return window had begun. For the later-month edge case, CRSP price, shares, and monthly return records existed before the holding start, but its dated name/exchange records did not place it in the selected exchange universe until 55 days after start; the first eligible cap was dated 60 days after start. The provider's midyear-entry rule can support a rank from that later entry, but cannot support assigning it to already elapsed holding months. A separate same-start-month case had no prior-December price/return and an eligible entry-month endpoint 28 days after month start; it remains a monthly proxy.

I changed `build.sas` to retain `rank_cap_date`, `cap_date_usable`, and `cap_date_class`, and to allow a first-cap fallback only when it predates or shares the holding-start month. The later-month category is counted but carries no target decile. In the rebuilt linked-accounting population, 50 later-month assignments are unavailable (with 239 before-start, 10 same-month, and 25,286 prior-December assignments; 412 other rows have no rank). These 50 are pre-return-window qualification counts, not final-row attrition. The target-window completeness and pooled-trim pipeline were rerun: 25,198 rows complete before trimming, 502 strict lower/upper-tail rows removed using the same pooled 1st/99th percentile procedure, and final N=24,696. Compared with A1, one old final key is removed and none is added; all three accounting variables are identical on the 24,696 common keys, and SAR differs by at most 2.49×10⁻¹⁴. The final file has no unusable cap date: 24,468 prior-December ranks, 227 before-start first-cap ranks, and one same-month proxy. ## Estimation and comparison

The paper body on physical p. 13 stars the return-equation intercept, while Table 2 and its parameter list on physical p. 14 use one shared unstarred \(\omega_0\). The implementation follows Table 2. Because a recursive system with unrestricted cross-equation error covariance does not structurally identify the return slope, the Stata cell uses the authorized best-effort independent-error conditional Gaussian likelihood with two positive error standard deviations. The unrestricted fit is checked against forecast OLS and conditional return OLS. Its maximum structural-to-OLS mapping gap is 3.83×10⁻⁶ absolute and 2.10×10⁻⁶ scale-relative. All coefficient standard errors are finite; estimated forecast and return error SDs are 0.237086 and 0.972162.

The nested likelihood tests are estimated natively on the same 24,696 observations, each with one restriction. The local \(\omega_1=\omega_1^*\) result is LR=4.37, p=0.0366, against the paper's 3.81, p=0.05. The local \(\omega_2=\omega_2^*\) result is displayed as 0.00 at the exports' two-decimal LR precision, p=0.9631, against the paper's 12.06, p<0.001. No SSR-ratio approximation or fabricated R² is reported. The export parentheses are two-tailed p-values; the source table reports asymptotic standard errors. Exact local coefficient estimates and standard errors are summarized in `design.md`, alongside the source values.

The final descriptive sample has N=24,696. Local mean/median EARN is 0.0060/0.0416, compared with the paper's approximate 0.00/0.04; local mean/median BKLG is 0.5005/0.3120, compared with 0.46/0.30. The distribution uses the actual joint-analysis rows. The highest SAR remains in the sample because the paper trims backlog, not returns.

The sample is 2,805 rows above the source's 21,891. The data vintage and approximate benchmark are plausible contributors, but the source does not provide enough intermediate counts to attribute the full difference to any one source. The source's weighting function and covariance restriction are also unspecified. Coefficients and especially the backlog LR result therefore remain materially different rather than being treated as a close numerical reproduction.

## Remaining limitations

- CRSP target membership/tie and midyear-entry details are reconstructed from monthly data; MSIX is value-weighted and annually rebalanced, while the paper describes a mean-return buy-and-hold portfolio. The same-month endpoint is a monthly timing proxy.
- Calendar-year versus fiscal-year formation can differ for non-December fiscal years.
- One dated CCM PERMNO/PERMCO change and eight expired FYE links remain as specifically counted identity limitations; no cause is imputed to the PERMCO change.
- Current data vintage coverage and the paper's unspecified weighted estimator prevent exact sample and coefficient replication. These do not justify tuning sample rules to published N or estimates.
