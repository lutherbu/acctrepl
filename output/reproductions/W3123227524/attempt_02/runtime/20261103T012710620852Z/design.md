# Table 6 reproduction design

## Scope and source evidence

This attempt estimates the last full-control specification in Table 6 Panels A–F: A[6], B[7], C[7], D[7], E[7], and F[7]. The source reports mean coefficients and Fama–MacBeth t-statistics from annual cross-sections, with DROA in year 1 regressed on earnings-management measures from year 0 plus sales and capital-expenditure growth. Panel A uses DA; Panels B–D add abnormal CFO, production costs, and discretionary expenses respectively; Panels E–F add RM_1 and RM_2 respectively. Source: printed pp. 16–17 (PDF pp. 15–16), Table 6 and its notes.

The sample paragraph on printed p. 6 (PDF p. 5) specifies completed U.S. common-stock offers by U.S. issuers listed on NYSE, NASDAQ, or AMEX, during 1987–2006. It excludes low-price completed offers, withdrawn offers with low filing midpoint, spin-offs, reverse LBOs, closed-end funds, UITs, REITs, limited partnerships, rights and standby issues, simultaneous or combined security offers, nondomestic issues, and offers without the required Compustat statements. This construction retains each `MASTER_DEAL_NO` as an event; it does not collapse multiple offers by an issuer in one year.

The local catalog confirms the required fields in `SDC.WRDS_NI_DETAILS` and `COMP.FUNDA`. Observed SDC codes are `SECURITY='Common Shares'`, `IPO='No'`, `NATION='United States'`, `CURRENCY='US'`, and `LISTMAIN` values `NYSE`, `NASDQ` (the local NASDAQ code), and `AMEX`. The registration-status field is blank for 608,102 of 608,783 event-period records; the nonblank values are `RULE144A`, `RULE415`, and `SECREG`. It therefore does not identify completed deals. `MASTER_DEAL_DATE` is catalog-labeled Issue Date; `FILINGDATE` is a distinct filing date used for the four-statement screen. Completion is approximated by an observed issue date and no postponed/withdrawn dates (`POSTPONED_DATE`, `WITHDRAWN_DATE`, `WITHDRAWN_DATE2`). The offer-price string is parsed as a numeric amount and the $5 screen is applied in the SDC currency field used by the paper’s U.S. offers. SDC `YEAR` agrees with the issue-date year among target-market candidates; the issue date determines the event year. The local catalog does not expose exact deal-category codes for the paper’s rights/standby, unit, fund, and reverse-LBO exclusions, so those exclusions use description/business text matches as a best-effort screen. The separate `REGRIGHTS` field is labeled Registration Rights and is not treated as an issue-type code.

The provider item crosswalk and local Compustat guide verify item 123 as `IBC`, item 308 as `OANCF`, item 124 as `XIDOC`, item 6 as `AT`, item 7 as gross `PPEGT`, item 12 as `SALE`, item 41 as `COGS`, item 3 as `INVT`, items 45/46 as `XAD`/`XRD`, and item 189 as `XSGA`. The legacy-variable mapping is also consistent with the exact local catalog labels. No price-per-share or share-count variable enters the assigned regressions; split adjustment is not applicable to these accounting totals and ratios.

## Issuer links, periods, currencies, and sample construction

The SAS build reads only the required fields and FY1981–2007 annual records from the exact catalog-listed tables. FY1981 is included for the pre-filing statement screen; the `INDL/C/STD/D/USA` row filters otherwise remain. Historical SIC is `SICH`. Current-year (t) auxiliary-support and SEO-analysis observations require a valid nonfinancial SIC excluding 6000–6999. The full nonfinancial Compustat population, not the SEO-only sample, supplies the two-digit-SIC/fiscal-year normal-level regressions. The selected statement population also supplies t−1 and t−2 accounting inputs for the current-year equations, without requiring a usable SIC in those prior years, and t+1 `IB` for the outcome, without requiring t+1 SIC or t+1 proxy estimation. The observed annual analysis rows cover offer-year 1987–2006 and t+1 outcomes through FY2007. Among the FY1982–2007 nonfinancial rows, 135,378 are USD and 183 are CAD. Ratios/differences in the auxiliary models require adjacent-year currency agreement. Issuer/control size-match assets and the cross-period ROA inputs require USD at t−1, t, and t+1; no currency conversion is inferred.

SDC offer-date CUSIP is linked to dated `COMP.SEC_IDHIST` CUSIP rows (`EFFFROM`–`EFFTHRU`), rather than to `COMP.FUNDA.CUSIP`: WRDS documents that legacy Compustat CUSIP was historically the most recent identifier, while `SEC_IDHIST` contains historical IDs that change over time ([WRDS historical identifier guide](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/wrds-research/database-linking-matrix/using-compustat-historical-identifier-notebook/)). The build restricts the history to records whose effective interval overlaps 1987–2006 and checks both open-boundary special missing values and ordinary missing dates. The event-date match uses inclusive effective dates; an exact 9-character CUSIP is preferred when present, otherwise a dated 6-character CUSIP match is used. Multiple GVKEYs remaining after that preference are ambiguous and excluded. The match is at the security-history row level and does not infer a primary-security marker. The nonissuer screen applies the same date-effective CUSIP-to-GVKEY crosswalk to all observed U.S. common-share IPO and SEO issues in 1987–2006; IPO/SEO issuers with no dated link are not classified as issuers in Compustat. The local standard U.S. FUNDA annual source had two duplicate GVKEY–FYEAR groups (GVKEY 006557 in FY1982–1983) with different DATADATEs but the same period duration and final/update flags; within an annual fiscal-year key the latest DATADATE is retained for annual financial variables. This annual duplicate rule is separate from the historical CUSIP linkage.

Printed p. 6 also requires “statement data for the 4 years prior to the SEO filing date” and says each retained firm-year must have data to calculate the discretionary-accrual and real-earnings-management proxies. The filing-history screen uses the latest dated annual record for each standard Compustat GVKEY–FYEAR after `INDL/C/STD/D/USA` selection and before the historical-SIC screen. Each selected annual DATADATE must precede the event's `FILINGDATE`. The implementation requires four consecutive FYEAR labels ending with the latest FYEAR available before that filing date. The source does not spell out a gap rule; requiring consecutive labels avoids allowing remote or separated old statements to stand in for the four preceding years. The raw Compustat read begins in FY1981 so the four-year window is available for the earliest observed 1985 filing dates. This test does not require prior-year OANCF/XIDOC, a usable historical SIC, or prior-year proxy calculations. A2 corrects an A1 defect that imposed valid nonfinancial `SICH` while counting the required prior statements. Valid nonfinancial `SICH` remains required for the current analysis and industry-year support population.

The p. 6 data-availability sentence is implemented as a common issuer year-0 screen for all six Table 6 panels: the issuer must have calculated year-0 DA, abnormal CFO, abnormal production costs, abnormal discretionary expenses, RM_1, and RM_2. RM measures are derived from their required abnormal components. This is an event-level issuer rule; the matched nonissuer control only needs year-0 and year-1 ROA plus prior assets for the size/performance match. The auxiliary-population regressions remain equation-specific: each uses the full eligible nonfinancial Compustat pool and its own complete input rows, minimum eight observations, and full-rank check. This reads “each firm-year observation” as a common proxy-available SEO sample, while preserving the separate support requirements of the four auxiliary equations. Year 0 is aligned to the calendar year of `MASTER_DEAL_DATE` and Compustat `FYEAR`; year 1 is the next FYEAR. The six Table 6 cells then apply complete-case filtering for their shared dependent variable, growth controls, and focal variable.

For the post-SEO outcome, ROA is `IB_t/AT_{t-1}`. Each event is matched to a Compustat nonissuer with prior-year assets within 90%–110% of issuer assets, selecting the smallest absolute year-0 ROA gap. There is no industry restriction, and a control may be reused. A control is a Compustat GVKEY with no mapped completed U.S. common-stock IPO or SEO in 1987–2006; this issuer-history screen uses all SDC IPO and SEO event records, without imposing the target sample’s $5/listing screens. Exact ties go to ascending GVKEY. The source specifies neither the ROA match year nor tie-breaking or nonreuse rules; year 0, ascending GVKEY, and reuse are best-effort choices. The dependent variable is `(ROA_issuer,t+1 − ROA_issuer,t) − (ROA_control,t+1 − ROA_control,t)`.

## Measures and estimators

Total accruals use the source definition `TA=IBC−(OANCF−XIDOC)`. XIDOC remains missing when not observed. DA is the residual from the Jones cross-sectional normal-accrual model with `TA/AT_{t-1}` as the dependent variable and `1/AT_{t-1}`, `ΔSALE/AT_{t-1}`, and gross `PPEGT/AT_{t-1}` as regressors. The support model has no ordinary constant.

The normal CFO, production-cost, and discretionary-expense models follow printed pp. 7–9 (PDF pp. 6–8): CFO is `OANCF−XIDOC`; PROD is `COGS+(INVT_t−INVT_{t-1})`; DISX is `XAD+XRD+XSGA`. Missing XAD and XRD are set to zero only when XSGA is observed; if XSGA is missing, DISX is missing. Each equation is scaled by beginning assets and has no ordinary constant. Eligible equation-specific two-digit-SIC/FYEAR groups require at least eight complete observations and full regressor rank. PROC REG ANOVA model degrees of freedom supply the rank check; invalid/singular groups do not supply residuals. RM_1 is `R_PROD−R_DISX`; RM_2 is `−R_CFO−R_DISX`.

The Table 6 growth controls are not defined arithmetically by the paper. They are measured as `(SALE_t−SALE_{t−1})/SALE_{t−1}` and `(CAPX_t−CAPX_{t−1})/CAPX_{t−1}`; missing/nonpositive prior denominators yield missing growth. The Table 6 constant convention is also unstated. The annual cross-sections include a constant and it is hidden from exported rows; no weights, fixed effects, or added clusters are used. The installed `xtfmb` estimator reports Fama–MacBeth coefficients with its default full covariance (no Newey–West lag), and the template exports two-sided p-values in parentheses. The source reports t-statistics; the significance presentation can differ because the template uses two-sided p-values.

## Audit and result interpretation

The bounded 1987–1989 audit finds no `.B`/`.E` filing-date sentinel and no filing date after issue among the local U.S. common-share exchange candidates. Across linked target offers, the median issue-minus-filing intervals are 23, 22, and 28 days for 1987, 1988, and 1989. Broad Compustat FUNDA history contains at least four pre-filing FYEARs for 115 of 173 uniquely linked 1987 candidates, 42 of 70 1988 candidates, and 92 of 130 1989 candidates. The A1 zero counts arose because the four-statement join used `comp_annual` after applying valid/nonfinancial `SICH`; on the exact 1982–1989 production rows, 113/115, 42/42, and 92/92 linked candidates still had at least four pre-filing FYEARs after the file-format/country filters, but none did after requiring valid nonfinancial SIC. The A2 build keeps this statement-availability test separate from the historical SIC screen. The audit script and aggregate logs are under `audit/`.

The A1 handoff had 2,351 distinct offers from 1990–2006. That sample is superseded after correcting the prior-statement SIC restriction; the final A2 event count, year coverage, complete-case Ns, and annual ranks are reported below after the rebuild. The original window remains 1987–2006, and no target count is imposed. The separate native annual-rank check retains only full-rank Table 6 cross-sections; the underidentified 1990 D/E/F groups remain omitted if still underidentified in the final handoff.
