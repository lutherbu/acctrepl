## Independent event, identity, and timing checks

I rebuilt the first material report and later explicit clean report from the Audit Analytics SOX 302 fields, then checked them against the 45 firms in the reused handoff. The code collapses issue rows by CIK, form, filing date, and period end; any explicit `MATERIAL_WEAKNESS=1` makes a filing material, while a clean filing requires at least one explicit zero and no positive or unknown value. `IS_EFFECTIVE` remains separate and does not define materiality or correction. The first material filing is selected from September 2002 through July 2008; the first later explicit clean filing is allowed through December 2008. The independent source rebuild found 3,775 CIK events. Every final firm has one handoff first-event date and at most one clean date; the 45 handoff first dates and all nonmissing clean dates match the rebuilt source dates. Three firms have no later explicit clean report by December 2008, matching the source rebuild. The audit is in [event_identity_timing_audit.sas](runtime/event_identity_timing_audit.sas) and its aggregate log/list outputs are alongside it.

Appendix A (printed p. 132) explicitly defines Uncorrected as running from the first material-weakness day through the day of the first report showing correction, so using filing dates and including the clean-report date follows the paper. Corrected starts the following day to keep the two periods disjoint; the paper says “the three-year period following” correction but does not specify the exact anniversary-day convention. The build uses the three-year calendar anniversary inclusively, and uses the first-report anniversary as the inclusive start of the prior period. These are transparent date-boundary choices. In the 223-facility handoff, a native SAS recomputation from `DEALSCAN.FACILITY.FacilityStartDate` matched every prior/uncorrected/corrected flag, with zero missing dates, zero loan-year mismatches, and every row assigned to exactly one period (108 prior, 37 uncorrected, 78 corrected). No facility starts on the prior-period start, first-material filing date, clean-report date, day after a clean report, or corrected-period anniversary, so this sample confirms the construction but does not exercise those exact boundaries.

The historical borrower routes use dated facility-time evidence. A ticker route requires the facility ticker to match the dated CRSP ticker, facility borrower name to equal contemporaneous `IssuerNm` after case/punctuation normalization, and a date-valid CCM LC/LU link; one exact-name P link is preferred, and a single C link is accepted only if no P link exists. The independent LEI fallback requires a unique LEI and borrower name across the LoanConnector tranche bridge (which can contain multiple tranche IDs), a unique direct Revelio company/RCID/GVKEY/CIK for that LEI, an exact match between the LoanConnector borrower name and the direct company name, and a CIK-to-GVKEY match in `COMP.NAMES` for the facility year. The Revelio ultimate-parent fields are carried for diagnostics but never used to select the borrower. Unique routes that disagree are left unmatched. Among the 223 final facilities, 93 are tagged `both_agree`, 72 use only the dated name-confirmed CRSP route, and 58 use the direct LEI/CIK/name fallback; no parent-only or conflicting route is present in the selected sample. The `both_agree` tag denotes agreement on GVKEY when the LEI has one GVKEY; the independently name/CIK-qualified LEI fallback is the 58-row `lei_cik_name` route. This validates the implemented predicates and final route provenance, not the true identity of excluded unmatched borrowers.

Finally, I independently rebuilt the annual controls from `COMP.FUNDA` using the implemented calendar-year t−1 rule: consolidated standardized industrial USD records with positive assets, exclusion of duplicated GVKEY/calendar-year/DATADATE keys, then the latest unique DATADATE within the preceding calendar year. The four resulting measures (log assets, EBITDA/assets, long-term debt/assets, and (net PPE + inventory)/assets) match the handoff for all 223 facilities. All have one eligible prior-calendar-year source record; none has the wrong calendar year or a source date after facility start. The selected record’s age is 6–697 days (median 213). Appendix A says “the year prior to entering into a loan contract” but does not say whether this means the preceding calendar year or the latest fiscal report available before signing; the build’s calendar-year interpretation is an operational choice and can leave a record up to 697 days old. No further timing exclusion is supported by the source.

## Samples, estimates, and native diagnostics

The main sample attrition is large. Audit Analytics yields 3,775 first-event company CIKs, 2,298 of which map uniquely to Compustat for the report year. The facility-mapping diagnostic counts 11,356 mapped facilities out of 133,309 dated DealScan facilities overall; that denominator includes unrelated borrowers and is not an ICW linkage rate. Among 2,510 facilities attached to mapped ICW firms, 1,645 fall in event windows. Of these, 738 have a rating score and 907 do not; the latter include unavailable mapping/history and conflict states, not only confirmed unrated firms. Shared controls retain 414 facilities across 126 firms, then the complete-case prior/post screen retains 223 across 45 firms. These losses are consistent with conservative historical borrower and rating coverage; the available history does not establish that every excluded facility should have mapped. I found no source-linked identifier or date predicate that demonstrably explains all of the reduction, and did not relax a screen to approach the published N.
