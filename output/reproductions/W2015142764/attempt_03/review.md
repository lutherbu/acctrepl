## Repairs

### Accounting currency and market equity

A2 computed accruals and ratios from nominal Compustat amounts without checking the selected row's reporting currency, then paired those values with CRSP market capitalization in USD. This can mix currencies in TCA, CFO, cross-year averages and changes, the five-year CFO variance, and book-to-market. In A3, the selected `COMP.FUNDA` row's own `CURCD` and `DATADATE` determine every conversion; no currency descriptor is transferred across years.

The implementation follows the Xpressfeed cross-rate procedure in the cached 2011 provider guide (physical PDF pp. 82–89; printed pp. 74–81; formula at physical p. 87 / printed p. 79). For each non-USD, non-GBP reporting currency, it computes `source amount / EXRAT(GBP→CURCD) × EXRAT(GBP→USD)` on the same date. It uses `EXRATD` for balance-sheet items and `EXRAT12M` for income-statement and cash-flow items. USD amounts use the identity conversion; GBP amounts require only the GBP→USD leg. Both legs must be positive, unique, and observed for that date. Missing or duplicate legs leave the converted amount missing. Daily `EXRATTPD` is carried with each leg as provenance; blank or different types do not block a positive quote.

Each period's ACT, LCT, CHE, DLC, RECT, PPEGT, AT, DLTT, and LT is translated with its own daily rate before changes are taken. IB, DP, and SALE are translated with each row's flow rate before CFO, TA, revenue changes, and CFO/asset ratios are computed. Average assets use current and preceding USD assets. CFO at t−1, t, and t+1 is already in USD before the t-year denominator is applied. The resulting ratios have consistent USD numerator and denominator units. The positive-rate catalogs contain 28,147 monthly and 797,870 daily date/currency keys; neither has a duplicate positive key. The full selected Compustat coverage summary records 8,469 missing non-USD flow conversions and 8,482 missing stock conversions before FYEAR 1982, plus 200 and 760 missing conversions respectively at/after 1982. These are coverage counts over the selected accounting history, not counts of dropped baseline rows. No rate is imputed.

CRSP price/share market equity is already USD: `abs(PRC)*SHROUT/1000` is USD millions. A2 paired consolidated accounts with one selected PERMNO's security value. A3 instead checks the effective CCM LPERMCO at the three-month valuation date, requires one PERMCO and one GVKEY mapping, identifies all active NYSE/AMEX/NASDAQ common issues for that PERMCO, and sums their price/share values on one common date with complete issue quotes within 30 days of the target. It leaves MVE missing for ambiguous mappings or incomplete quote sets. Out of 329,019 selected firm-years, 208,501 had a unique effective company mapping; 212 PERMCO/date mappings overlapped multiple GVKEYs; 120,306 had no effective PERMCO. There were 174,772 final company market values, including 1,623 values with multiple common issues. No PERMNO/date duplicates or DSF/DSENAMES PERMCO mismatches were found. SIZE is the natural log of USD MVE in millions; BM divides USD book equity by USD MVE.

### Supporting-regression rank

A nonmissing coefficient or residual does not establish that a fit is full rank. Each native PROC REG fit now requests OUTEST `EDF`; rank is complete-case N minus residual degrees of freedom. The required ranks are six for the no-intercept DD fit, four for the no-intercept ABACC fit, and four for the daily factor fit with its intercept. A fit that fails its rank requirement has all residuals set missing, which then flows into DD's unchanged five-residual window or the daily variance construction. No additional minimum-N screen was introduced; the 20-firm industry-year threshold is unchanged.

The native run found 1,197 eligible DD industry-years with 99,850 support rows; all 1,197 had rank six, and the residual file had zero missing residuals after the rank gate. It found 1,228 eligible ABACC industry-years with 116,054 support rows; all had rank four, with zero missing residuals after the rank gate. Of 21,103 daily factor fits, 21,091 had rank four and 12 were rank deficient; their residuals were propagated as missing. The CRSP PERMNO/date duplicate count was zero. The rank calculation and EDF field follow the [SAS/STAT PROC REG documentation](https://support.sas.com/documentation/onlinedoc/stat/151/reg.pdf).

Before the full construction, a bounded native SAS fixture checked unequal currency legs, USD and GBP handling, blank/mixed type labels, complete versus incomplete multi-issue USD market values, and full versus singular PROC REG fits. The conversion factors were 1.12 and 1.25 in the EUR examples; the full-rank no-intercept fit produced rank three versus rank two for the collinear fit, the factor model produced rank four versus three, and the rank gate blanked all singular-fit residuals. The two-issue example summed to USD 35 million, while the incomplete two-issue quote set was omitted. The catalog-path checks for both Xpressfeed rate tables passed.

## Limits

The paper does not report its instrument set, GMM weighting details, or panel covariance ordering. The best-effort moment set uses the included RHS variables as their own instruments, giving an exactly identified linear system whose point solution equals OLS; `ivreg2` reports a panel Bartlett HAC covariance with three lags after `xtset firmid fyear`. It gives z statistics and two-sided normal p-values; the source table gives t-statistics. The paper also does not specify the average-assets date convention, market-equity share classes, or rate quote date. The local same-date USD conversions and PERMCO company-market-value rule are documented best-effort choices. Unavailable foreign currency legs and ambiguous/incomplete market-value mappings remain missing. Missing analyst and institutional observations also remain unfilled. The local samples remain smaller than the published samples, and the exact causes are not established by the available diagnostics.
