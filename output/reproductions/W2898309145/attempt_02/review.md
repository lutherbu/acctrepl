## Scope and source check

The frozen assignment is Table 6, Panel C, All firms: pooled OLS of one-year Δ/MVA1 on a constant and `TIME = FYEAR − 1988`, for FY1988–2014, retaining firms with at least five eligible rows (not necessarily consecutive). The source reports firm-and-year clustered standard errors, no fixed effects, N=111,558, an intercept of 0.0103 (t=8.34), a TIME slope of 0.0001 (t=1.15), and adjusted R²=0.0003 (physical p. 24). The attempted cell implements that scope with `ivreg2`, two-way firm/year clustering, and `small`; it adds no fixed effects or sign restriction.

I checked the original PDF at physical pp. 7–8, 10, 13, 15–16, and 24. The body on p. 7 describes TXR as a tax refund receivable and defines adjusted cash taxes as TXPD less the change in TXR. The provider’s *Research Insight North America Data Guide* (2004), physical p. 153 / printed p. 103, likewise defines TXR/A161 as an amount due from the IRS, including refund claims and recoverable income taxes. No missing-to-zero rule appears there. The build requires current TXR and prior-year TXR to be observed and does not substitute reserves or UTP data. The source’s “1998 through 1992” rate range is descending and conflicts with its 1988 sample start; using 34% for FY1988–92 and 35% for FY1993–14 is a documented reading of that apparent typo. Table 4’s generic `.35 × PI` shorthand is noted as a conflict.

Table 1 (physical p. 10) defines MVA as AT + (PRCC_Q × CSHO − SEQ); Table 4 (p. 16) specifies beginning MVA and replacement with beginning BVA when MVA is missing. The build uses the prior fiscal-year AT, CSHO, and SEQ and a PRCCQ row at that exact DATADATE. It identifies the dated primary issue through HIID and accepts SEC_MTHPRC.CURCDM as the price currency only where same-GVKEY/HIID/date PRCCM agrees with PRCCQ within one cent. Missing MVA inputs invoke the stated beginning-AT fallback; an observed zero is preserved and then excluded, consistent with Table 3 (p. 15). Price-match diagnostics show agreement counts and price-difference summaries. The fallback is therefore source-authorized for unavailable MVA construction, not a zero fill.

The CTE calculation follows the Table 4 note on physical p. 16: TXFED+TXFO when both are available; otherwise TXT minus TXDI, TXS, and TXO, with missing values of those three components set to zero only in that fallback. The sample requires nonmissing CTE and current AT as indicated by Table 3; the measure also needs beginning AT for its denominator/fallback. Neither PI nor CTE is restricted by sign. The SAS diagnostics retain both signs in the common sample (before the five-row firm rule: 41,624 negative PI and 76,915 positive PI; 13,527 negative CTE and 81,350 positive CTE, with zeros also retained). The Table 6 note’s five-observation rule is applied after constructing and winsorizing the common sample.

The source’s incorporation/status/name fields have temporal limits. It requests FINC=0 (footnote 14, physical p. 13), but the exact FUNDA/COMPANY catalog has no FINC; the current FUNDA FIC field is labeled “Current ISO Country Code - Incorporation.” The 2004 provider guide maps legacy FIC code 0 to U.S. incorporation (physical p. 78), so `FIC='USA'` is a current-header approximation, not a historical FINC match. COMPANY.STKO is likewise current/undated; the provider guide defines 1/2 as subsidiary codes (physical pp. 120–121), and the build uses the exclusions and STKO=0 missing-share-price rule from footnote 14, while retaining the Table 3 wording conflict on p. 15. FUNDA.CONM is not dated, so name screens are current/header approximations. CUSIP uses effective-dated SEC_IDHIST where a unique match exists, otherwise the recorded FUNDA/header fallback; the build reports both routes. These are material historical-classification limitations.

## Construction and currency audit

No demonstrated construction defect required a repair. The build’s annual key joins preserve gaps, the prior TXR is not filled, and the MVA formula uses beginning values. It requires current AT and observed CTE availability, but retains loss and negative-CTE rows. Compustat reports FY2014 rows with actual DATADATEs as late as 31May2015; the SAS listing confirms that endpoint, and the rate-source date filters extend through 31Dec2015. A bounded read of the reused final handoff confirms that its 2,593 retained FY2014 rows span 30Jun2014–31May2015; all have stable native reporting currency, so none requires FX conversion. This is not a claim that every candidate mixed-currency record has a usable rate.

The currency logic preserves Δ in native reporting currency when current and prior CURCD agree. If the beginning price currency differs, it converts only that price at the beginning date to the beginning statement currency. At the final handoff grain, 12 rows overall use `delta_route=USD_CROSS`. Separately, the four retained rows with a non-unit price-to-beginning conversion split into three stable-reporting rows with `delta_route=NATIVE` and one row using `USD_CROSS`. This confirms the price conversion does not rewrite a stable-currency flow. Only mixed reporting histories translate the required flows through EXRAT12M and stocks through EXRATD to a common USD basis. The arithmetic follows the Xpressfeed 2011 guide, physical pp. 83–89: convert reporting currency to primary GBP using the inverse GBP-to-reporting rate, then apply GBP-to-target; exact positive unique quote keys are used. The implementation does not require EXRATTPD provenance equality or actual-only rates. At the earlier `work.candidate` source-candidate grain (302,831 rows, before the final population and firm-history filters), A1’s native construction listing reports 314 mixed-reporting-history candidates, of which 150 have a complete USD route; it also reports 541 candidate rows with an available beginning-price currency conversion. These are diagnostics, not imposed sample targets.

The paper says Δ/MVA measures are winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles (Table 4 note, physical p. 16), but does not specify pooled versus year-specific cutoffs, algorithm, or whether cutoffs precede the Table 6 firm-history rule. The retained best-effort choice is pooled PCTLDEF=5 cutoffs on the one-year common-eligible sample before the ≥5-row firm rule. It is disclosed and not selected to match the published coefficients.
