## Repairs and construction checks

The A1 build had corrected PTBI scaling by its current version, and A2 independently verified that both the dependent variable and current PTBI predictor are scaled. The best-effort denominator timing is mean AT at t-1 and t for PTBI_t, and mean AT at t and t+1 for PTBI_t+1; the paper says average total assets but does not specify this interval. All three annual asset values used across the two averages must be observed and positive. Currency is checked at t-1, t, and t+1, so no accounting amount is divided by assets reported in a different currency.

PTBI is PI less MII when MII is reported and PI otherwise, following footnote 18. The final sample has 2,172 missing MII observations at t and 2,243 at t+1; 11,329 observations report MII as exactly zero at t. Missing MII is preserved and flagged; the code does not replace a missing item with an observed zero. It uses PI when item #49 is absent, consistent with the paper's conditional wording. The current-PTBI loss screen is strictly below zero; it retains three exact-zero adjusted PTBI rows. Raw PI below zero flags 12,161 complete rows, compared with 12,177 after the paper's MII adjustment, so the choice changes 16 loss flags in this local sample.

The DTE construction uses federal plus foreign deferred taxes when both are reported, otherwise total deferred taxes TXDI; state deferred taxes are excluded. The amount is grossed up at 35% and scaled by average assets at t. LNBTD and LPBTD are formed from the bottom and top quintiles of un-winsorized scaled BTD within each fiscal year. PROC RANK GROUPS=5 retains its default mean-rank handling for ties; the local bottom and top groups each contain 3,120 of 15,609 observations. The code then applies pooled 1st/99th percentile resets to PTBI_t, PTBI_t+1, and scaled BTD using the eligible analysis sample. The paper does not specify pooled versus annual cutoffs. Group sizes are results of this procedure, not targets.

### Current-tax correction

A1 screened on the newer TXC field and dropped rows when TXC was missing. The paper's Table 2 note defines current tax as item #16 minus item #50, which the provider guide maps to TXT minus TXDI. A2 now uses `TXT - TXDI` for the negative-current-tax screen and retains missing formula inputs as an explicit unknown status rather than coding them to zero. This missing-value policy is best effort because the paper does not specify whether unknown current-tax screen values should be dropped.

The field comparison supports following the paper formula. Among 69,578 final-update COMP.FUNDA rows with TXT, TXDI, and TXC observed, TXC equals TXT-TXDI within $0.0001 million for 65,951 (94.79%) and differs for 3,627 (5.21%). Differences range from -$848.4 million to $1,357.0 million. The A1 screen removed 2,525 rows at the current-tax missingness step; the paper formula is unavailable for 117 rows after the Equation (2) construction and currency screens, and 29 of these remain in the final sample with unknown tax status. Requiring known tax status would remove those 29; no alternative regression was estimated.

### UPD and NOL status

The UPD=3 filter is not prescribed in the article. It is retained as a documented provider-status choice: the 2004 guide defines UPD=1 as not updated apart from adjustment/market data, UPD=2 as preliminary, and UPD=3 as final (physical page 122 / printed 72). Of 109,696 standard annual COMP.FUNDA rows with fiscal labels 1993–2001, 5,526 are UPD=1, 535 are UPD=2, and 103,635 are UPD=3. The 6,061 raw rows excluded are mostly not final accounting records, with 535 preliminary records. Using final reports is reasonable for finalized annual items, but it can exclude usable preliminaries when a final source is unavailable. The paper does not resolve the choice. Its effect is disclosed as a 5.5% raw-row reduction; the filter was not adopted to bring the sample near the published N.

The current catalog exposes TLCF, not a verified NOL flag. The build excludes 3,912 rows with observed positive TLCF and retains 5,687 final rows with missing TLCF as unknown, not zero. The local positive-TLCF count differs from the paper's 3,194 Compustat-identified NOL exclusions; the current item cannot classify unknown values, so this screen remains approximate.

The CRSP intersection uses the exact CCMXPF_LINKTABLE fields GVKEY, LINKPRIM, LINKTYPE, LPERMNO, LINKDT, and LINKENDDT and DSF fields PERMNO and DATE. It accepts time-valid P/C primary markers and LC/LU links and requires an actual CRSP daily date in the twelve months ending at DATADATE. Link multiplicities are checked before reducing to firm-year membership; the build reports 245 firm-years with multiple link rows and 85 with multiple PERMNOs, then has zero duplicate final GVKEY-year keys. The source does not give exact link rules. Local counts are 49,358 firm-years before CRSP membership and 37,521 after it, versus 47,507 in the paper. This coverage gap is unresolved and is not attributed to data vintage without evidence.

## Sample and result comparison

The A2 screen sequence is: 37,521 CRSP-intersection rows; 5,147 missing Equation (2) constructions; 11 cross-period currency mismatches; 32,363 complete constructions with stable currency; 12,177 negative adjusted PTBI values; 665 observed negative TXT-TXDI values; and 3,912 positive TLCF values. The final sample is N=15,609 firm-years and 4,572 firms. It includes 5,687 unknown TLCF statuses and 29 unknown current-tax statuses. Final currencies are USD for 15,603 observations and CAD for six. There are no duplicate final keys.

The source reports N=14,106 and 4,048 firms, so the local final N is 1,503 firm-years and 524 firms higher. Its listed exclusions are 22,242 missing regression variables, 6,447 pre-tax losses, 1,518 negative current taxes, and 3,194 NOLs. A2's missing-variable count covers only the assigned Equation (2) constructions, chosen average-assets timing, and currency continuity; the article does not enumerate all fields behind its broader missing-regression screen. The remaining sample-size difference is not explained by a specific cause.

The final interacted OLS fit is reported in [table3_panel_b.md](tables/table3_panel_b.md), with all formats in `tables/`. In source order, local estimates (t-statistics) are: constant -0.0098 (-5.20), LNBTD 0.0176 (4.60), LPBTD 0.0175 (4.19), PTBI_t 0.7581 (58.86), PTBI_t×LNBTD -0.0904 (-4.09), and PTBI_t×LPBTD -0.2239 (-8.37). N=15,609, R-squared=0.2618, and adjusted R-squared=0.2616. The equality test is F(1,15603)=20.41, p=0.0000063.

Table 3 Panel B reports -0.003 (-1.62), 0.017 (4.43), 0.017 (4.00), 0.742 (57.35), -0.100 (-4.56), and -0.212 (-7.99), respectively; adjusted R-squared is 0.272 and the interaction equality test is F=15.01 (p=0.0001). Both local interactions remain negative and individually significant at p<0.001, while the local equality test is stronger. The local LNBTD interaction is less negative and the LPBTD interaction more negative than in the paper. The local intercept is significant while the paper's is not. The paper specifies two-tailed tests; local conventional OLS p-values are two-tailed as well. No covariance correction is reported, so inference uses Stata's conventional OLS standard errors.

The final sample descriptive table is [sample_descriptives.md](tables/sample_descriptives.md). Local PTBI_t+1 mean/SD/25th/median/75th percentiles are 0.0848/0.1282/0.0296/0.0877/0.1530, versus the source's 0.093/0.121/0.0368/0.093/0.157. Local PTBI_t values are 0.1271/0.0932/0.0594/0.1071/0.1711, versus 0.132/0.092/0.066/0.113/0.176. For scaled temporary BTD (the source's DTE), local values are 0.0013/0.0379/-0.0129/0.0000/0.0175, versus 0.001/0.037/-0.014/0.001/0.018. The means and dispersions are similar, while the quartiles reflect the larger local sample and construction choices.
