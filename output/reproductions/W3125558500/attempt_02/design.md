Link and name effective dates are applied; CRSP header SIC is not substituted. A single valid dated code fills the exact industry. Records without a dated SIC are reported and are not dropped by a general CRSP-coverage requirement.

## Beginning market value, historical issue, and currency

Beginning MVE uses same-firm prior-FYEAR annual `CSHO × PRCC_F` (items 25 × 199), retaining the annual source fields as the production denominator. Research Insight 2004 describes CSHO as year-end shares outstanding (physical p. 196) and PRCC_F as the actual fiscal-year closing price (physical pp. 674–675); those definitions support the stipulated item construction but do not establish that this local FUNDA series was delivered through Research Insight or carries its display adjustments.

The prior annual `HIID` is verified against active `COMP.SEC_HISTORY` primary records: `ITEM` must be `PRIHISTUSA` or `PRIHISTCAN`, and the issue ID in `ITEMVALUE` must match the annual HIID for the same GVKEY and date. Ordinary missing `THRUDATE` is treated as an open interval. Across the entire SEC_HISTORY table, 177,600 rows have ordinary missing `THRUDATE`, with no user-missing end codes. The primary items have 53,311 PRIHISTUSA and 9,559 PRIHISTCAN rows, with no IID/ITEMVALUE disagreements in either item. A same-HIID date-matched `SECM` record marked P is a fallback only when the primary-history record is unavailable and a J marker does not contradict it. The code does not require company-wide uniqueness of P: a known HIID can be the valid U.S. or Canadian issue even when another issue is also marked P. The current header IID is not used as historical evidence.

All 124,572 final observations have dated primary-issue evidence: 124,221 have matching SEC_HISTORY and same-HIID SECM evidence, and 351 have SEC_HISTORY evidence alone. None has an HIID/SECM joiner-marker conflict. There are 2,533 final observations whose company-month has two P markers, retained because the specific annual HIID is verified. This resolves the A1 over-restriction without treating a generic history row as a primary assignment.

Price denomination comes from the same issue's daily `SEC_DPRC.CURCDD` when available, then same-issue monthly `SECM.CURCDM`. No annual FUNDA `CURCD` price fallback is used. The final handoff has 92,388 daily-denomination records and 32,184 monthly-denomination records. Among 91,978 final records with both daily and monthly currency codes, all codes agree. For annual `PRCC_F`, daily currency uses point-date `EXRATD` at the same-issue daily quote date; if only monthly `CURCDM` establishes denomination, `EXRATD` is joined at annual `DATADATE`. Monthly `EXRAT1M` is not used to convert the annual price. Annual flow values use same-month `EXRAT12M`. Same-currency earnings/MVE classifiers remain in native currency; only the 91 mixed-currency observations use the explicit common-USD approximation. All final flow FX conversions are supported.

The annual price remains `FUNDA.PRCC_F` even when it differs from daily or monthly security prices. On 92,388 final annual-price/daily-close pairs, 18,216 differ by more than $0.0001. The mismatch quote age has median 0 days and 99th percentile 3 days (maximum 30), so stale daily quotes are not a general explanation. Of those 18,216 mismatches, 17,855 match the same-issue monthly `PRCCM` within $0.0001. Annual `PRCC_F` matches monthly `PRCCM` within $0.0001 on 124,091 of 124,116 comparable final rows. Daily and monthly price levels agree within $0.0001 on 74,117 of 91,978 pairs, despite agreement of their currency codes on every paired record. These patterns point to a daily/monthly product-basis difference for most annual/daily mismatches, but do not prove the exact cause; the remaining differences stay unresolved.

Factor checks use the documented adjusted-close direction `PRCCD/AJEXDI` and `PRCCM/AJEXM` (Xpressfeed 2011, physical pp. 71–72). With the build's bounded tolerance of 0.05% or $0.0001, annual price is near adjusted daily close on 40,790 final rows and near adjusted monthly close on 62,373, compared with 74,172 annual/daily price pairs meeting the build's raw-price match rule and 124,091 annual/monthly pairs within $0.0001. This test does not support blind split rescaling or replacing annual PRCC_F. Research Insight 2004 documents special cases such as OTC bid prices and last trading prices on suspension, while Xpressfeed distinguishes raw price/share delivery from displayed values; the available local material does not prove the product basis of this FUNDA series or explain every remaining mismatch. The AJEX checks are diagnostics only.

## Sample construction and estimation

The source screen requires observed NI, PI, SPI, positive beginning MVE, a verified historical issue, supported annual flow currency conversion, and a usable classifier currency. PRESPC is `PI-SPI`. If current flow currency equals beginning-price currency, the three classifiers are formed directly in that native currency. When the currencies differ, flow and annual-price values are converted to a common USD basis using EXRAT12M and point-date EXRATD; the paper does not specify cross-currency timing, so these mixed-currency classifications are an explicit approximation.

For each fiscal year, SAS calculates 1st and 99th percentiles of NI/MVE, PRETAX/MVE, and PRESPC/MVE using `PCTLDEF=5`; records strictly outside any annual cutoff are deleted. The paper does not state a quantile definition or endpoint convention. Exact-zero NI, PRETAX, or PRESPC is removed only after annual tail trimming, consistent with footnote 10 (physical PDF p. 13). Here, 128,314 observations are eligible before tail deletion; 3,604 are trimmed, then 138 exact-zero records are removed, leaving 124,572. The handoff has unique `GVKEY,DATADATE` keys.

Table 4 uses undeflated amounts, while region membership is determined from scaled values (physical PDF p. 21). Panel A bins scaled PRETAX; Panel B bins scaled PRESPC. Small loss is `[-0.03,0)`, small profit `[0,0.03)`, loss is below −0.03, and profit is at or above 0.03. These boundaries represent six adjacent 0.005-wide intervals on each side of zero, with lower-inclusive/upper-exclusive endpoints. Region-specific regressors contain raw USD amounts within each region and zero elsewhere. The paper's footnote 17 mentions deleting statistical outliers but gives no rule; no unreported cutoff is invented.

Each panel is a pooled OLS regression with one common intercept and four region slopes. The paper does not name its covariance estimator; this attempt uses conventional pooled OLS standard errors, with no fixed effects or clustering. Native Stata `test` runs the two reported equality tests in each panel. The two regression cells and two actual-estimation-sample descriptive cells follow the notebook template and export `.md`, `.rtf`, `.csv`, and `.tex` files.
