- I_DATA_CLOSURE [unresolved; full_pipeline]: The imported master and analysis programs consume three separate author-prepared DTA panels, none of which is in the package inventory, and no supplied program produces them. The datasheet says the authors cannot share the received raw data under their terms, while describing the underlying provincial credit-registry, Bureau of Statistics and public location-indicator sources. The data-sharing restriction does not establish that this user lacks separate provider, on-site or licensed access. The package does not provide the three panel dictionaries/producers, loan-loss-allowance calculation, area-to-loan linkage, firm-to-loan linkage, or native panel keys/periods. A selected frozen catalog search does not prove equivalent access to those prepared contracts.
- I_MASTER_ROOT_SETUP [preparation; full_pipeline]: The supplied master leaves global root blank and constructs do-file paths from that value. The registered child scripts are under Code/, so root must resolve to that directory (or an equivalent staged child-code directory); Stata's working directory must also resolve the bare prepared-data filenames. This is a concrete setup prerequisite, not evidence that the child scripts are absent.

The analyses consume the loan outcomes, bank/firm identifiers, time and treatment fields, firm covariates, loan-loss-allowance changes, risk flag and documented location indicators. The provider-to-panel producer, native date/key contract, exact allowance-change construction, and geography mapping into loan records are not supplied.
- R_FIRM_PANEL [undetermined]: Required author-prepared firm-level panel firm_data_final.dta for table 1, table 9 and figure 3. The producer, firm/time keys, link to the loan records, treatment/exposure construction, and the native definitions of the risk/credit flags are not supplied.
- R_BANK_PANEL [undetermined]: Required author-prepared bank-level panel bank_data_final.dta for table 1. The producer, native bank/time keys, treatment mapping and definitions of the bank summary measures are not supplied.
- S_PSMATCH2 [preparation_required]: The bounded command metadata probe reports psmatch2 unavailable; table 7 requires its stated matching call.
- S_PSTEST [preparation_required]: The bounded command metadata probe reports pstest unavailable; table 7 requires its matching-balance call.
