The project

From published study to reproducible evidence

Five leading journals · published through 2020

The citation cohort contains 100 empirical papers published in 2000–2020 from each of The Accounting Review, Journal of Accounting Research, Journal of Accounting and Economics, Contemporary Accounting Research, and Review of Accounting Studies. The author-package cohort covers JAR volumes 51–58 (issue years 2013–2020). Nine of its 159 packages belong to papers also in the citation cohort. One paper has two separately archived packages, so the 650 catalog records cover 649 distinct DOIs.
