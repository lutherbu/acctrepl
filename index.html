The project

From published study to reproducible evidence

Five leading journals · original cohort 2000–2020

The original cohort contains 100 citation-selected empirical papers from each of The Accounting Review, Journal of Accounting Research, Journal of Accounting and Economics, Contemporary Accounting Research, and Review of Accounting Studies. The catalog also includes 407 downloaded JAR archive/material units from issue years 2013–2026, with nine overlaps linked to existing rows. Separate archive/correction units retain separate records. The sample and model records for 17 selected author papers identify their reading sources. The original 500 papers retain their metadata and citation ranks. Research artifacts and validation evidence are preserved alongside readable summaries.
